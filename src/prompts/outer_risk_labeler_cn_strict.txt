# prompts_ext/outer_risk_labeler_cn_strict.txt
Only generate risk categories based on 'evidenced clauses/terms' in the original contract text; Each category must be accompanied by 1-3 pieces of evidence (original text fragments).
If the evidence is insufficient, it is better to be lacking than excessive. New categories are allowed, but evidence must also be provided for the addition.
General categories (IP/data/confidentiality/SLA/breach/termination/dispute/jurisdiction/insurance, etc.) will not be output without evidence.
